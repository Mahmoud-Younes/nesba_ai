# AI Recipe Generator — Shape Brief

## Job, audience, and outcome
- Android UI for general users who want an alternative recipe for a meal they have already selected. Keep the request understandable without nutrition expertise. Visitor mode: Operate.
- This surface is **alternative only**. Do not create a recipe-from-scratch flow.
- The Shape direction is visual exploration. The selected composition is **Meal to alternative**: preserve a clear vertical progression from the original meal, through any stated preference context, to a visually distinct alternative recipe.

## Selected visual direction
- Inherit the team's selected **Bustan Light** identity and existing Material 3 conventions: IBM Plex Sans, established spacing rhythm, tonal surfaces, rounded components, clear hierarchy, and accessible Android controls.
- Use a single-column, scrollable mobile composition. Keep the original selected meal and alternative recipe explicitly labeled and visually distinguishable throughout the flow.
- Composition roll reference: `seed:2f2b7693eba35f98`.
- Use a neutral screen title such as **Alternative recipe** as a proposed copy direction; do not add a product name or wordmark.

## Proposed composition and content
1. **Original meal context:** A compact, clearly labeled card presents the existing selected meal as the starting point. Preserve supplied wording; do not infer or rewrite ingredients. Any sketch content is sample context, not a real selected meal. Do not add unrequested metadata.
2. **Preference context (optional):** Keep this section in the current screen, but show it only when explicit preference data is supplied to this flow. Hide the entire section when no preference data is available. Do not assume a saved user profile or persistent preferences. Do not add editable chips, checkboxes, or preference-selection controls in this UI phase. Any example preference labels used in a visual preview are illustrative only, not approved product options or taxonomy.
3. **Generate recipe:** A clear primary action appears after the meal and preference context. This is a UI-only preview action; it must never claim a recipe was actually generated by AI or personalized.
4. **Alternative recipe preview:** A visually distinct result section follows the action. Include a sample recipe title, illustrative ingredients, and illustrative preparation steps. Label the section and its contents as examples/visual preview, not generated from, calculated from, or personalized to the selected meal. Do not add nutrition figures by default; if figures are explored later, label each clearly illustrative and not calculated or verified.

## Interaction and states
- **Initial:** Show the original meal context and Generate recipe action. Show the non-editable preference-context section only when explicit preference data is supplied to this flow; otherwise hide the entire section. Do not fabricate a selected meal or saved preferences as real user data.
- **Preview interaction (proposed):** Activating Generate recipe may show a brief, visibly simulated preview/loading state and then reveal the illustrative sample recipe. Keep the preview label visible so the transition cannot be mistaken for real AI generation.
- **Recipe preview:** Keep original and alternative labels visible, including when scrolling; the alternative has its own visual treatment. Ingredients and steps remain clearly marked illustrative.
- No missing-meal validation, error/retry behavior, loading duration, result replacement behavior, or additional actions are defined by this brief. Decide those only if needed before implementation.

## Requirements and approval status
- **Documented baseline requirements:** `03-requirements.md` §3.3.1 lists FR-04 (generate customized recipes), FR-05 (consider dietary restrictions), FR-06 (suggest healthier alternatives), FR-11 (handle API/connection errors), and FR-15 (loading/error states) as related system requirements. This UI-only brief does not implement those service behaviors or establish that a real generation service is approved.
- **Documented use case:** `02-use-cases.md` §UC-02 `[E: S3 §3]` describes selecting a meal, requesting an alternative, including restrictions in an AI prompt, and displaying a recipe. Its proposed saving postcondition is `[P]` and is excluded here.
- **Proposed design details, not product approvals:** the screen title, card treatment, preference-context representation, preview transition, sample recipe content, and any loading presentation are design proposals for review.
- **Unresolved integration choices:** AI provider and request/response behavior remain subject to team decisions. `13-open-items.md` identifies OI-07 (API-key exposure/architecture) and P-16/P-17 (proposed schemas, timeout, and retry details); do not infer implementation from them. Persistence/data-layer ownership is unresolved at OI-05. Nutrition source OI-04 is not selected and is not needed for this UI exploration.

## Scope and constraints
- No API, backend, database, real AI generation, nutritional analysis, persistence, authentication, or production navigation.
- No Save action, allergen toggles, macro filters, health ratings, medical claims, or allergen-safety guarantees.
- No implication that the sample result is verified, calculated, personalized, or safe for a particular allergy or medical condition.
- Do not add a standalone recipe-from-scratch route or assume how the selected meal reaches this screen.
- Product name and localization remain unresolved. Do not choose a brand name, wordmark, language, translation, or localization policy.
- Keep the entry point and future destination unresolved.
- Follow Android safe-area, scrolling, keyboard (if any input is later approved), text scaling, screen-reader, RTL-readiness, and minimum 48dp touch-target conventions.

## Open decisions
- How a selected meal is supplied to this screen and whether its description alone is shown or other existing meal context is approved.
- How explicit dietary preference data is supplied to the screen and which labels are approved. The section is optional and conditional; editing controls and profile persistence are excluded from this UI phase.
- Whether the UI preview needs a simulated loading state, its timing/copy, and whether an illustrative error/retry state is desired.
- Approved recipe example content and whether any nutrition information belongs in this screen.
- Future AI provider, request/response schema, failure handling, security architecture, and persistence (including OI-04, OI-05, OI-07, P-16, and P-17).
- The production entry point, destination, product name, and localization decisions.
